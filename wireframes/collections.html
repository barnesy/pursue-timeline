Collections & Correlations — wireframe v1

Grab cases from anywhere into a Collection, tag them, then open Correlate to see how they relate in time, space, and across datasets — powered by the existing proximity engine (findNearby · km + years).

1 · GRAB
Add a case from the map, timeline, detail drawer, or search — one click, or multi-select.
2 · TAG
Label cases (“1947 wave”, “Nevada corridor”) to group within a collection.
3 · COLLECT
A tray holds your working set. Save / name / reopen collections.
4 · CORRELATE
Analyze the set: proximity network, matrix, mini-map links, timeline, findings.
